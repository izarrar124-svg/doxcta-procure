Pricing tier card. Doxcta sells outcomes, not hours — most tiers show a priceNote ("Scoped on discovery call") rather than a dollar figure. `highlighted` raises the card, tints it green, and adds a "Most popular" badge.
```jsx
<PricingCard name="Growth" description="Expanded support." price={null} priceNote="Most teams start here" idealFor="Founders with growing admin needs" features={["Executive assistance","Meeting coordination"]} highlighted index={1} />
```
